A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before you take off.

Can you help if this is my first trip to Europe?+

Yes. Start by telling us where you would like to go and what you need help with. We can discuss your route, flight planning, stays and Schengen visa preparation.

Does visa support guarantee approval?+

No. We help with preparation and the application process. The relevant embassy or consulate makes the visa decision.

Do I need fixed dates before getting in touch?+

No. You can start with an approximate month or simply an idea. Tell us how flexible you are so we can discuss a plan that suits you.
